Initial release of Redirect Guard.
- Stops unwanted ad redirects and unauthorized deep links to external applications.
- Displays a confirmation prompt to stay in your app or proceed to the target app.
- Safely terminates redirected applications using Shizuku.
- Supports both Ask Confirmation and Instant Auto-Return interception modes.
- Includes built-in shopping app presets and smart launcher whitelisting.
- Records intercepted redirect attempts in an activity log.
